Use cases

Where a small portable integrity record is useful, with its limits kept visible.

Documents

Bind a published statement or policy to exact bytes and a signing key.

Datasets

Identify a frozen dataset snapshot used for analysis or evaluation.

Models

Attach a signed record to model artifacts or cards.

Pipelines

Record the digest of a build output or release artifact.

Benchmarks

Bind a declared result set to its exact published bytes.

Agents

Publish an operating declaration without relying on a central GOVP account.

Suitability test

Use GOVP when recipients need a small, durable way to check who signed a record and whether supplied bytes match it. Add external systems when you also need identity certification, independent timestamps, revocation, availability or factual adjudication.
